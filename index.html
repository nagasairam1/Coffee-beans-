Our Story

Koraput: where the forest grows the coffee

Tucked into the Eastern Ghats of southern Odisha, Koraput is a landscape of dense forest, tribal villages, and altitude — the kind of terrain that, almost by accident, produces exceptional coffee. Long before "single-origin" became a marketing term, farmers here grew Arabica the only way they knew: under the shade of native trees, alongside turmeric and pepper, on small plots tended across generations.

We didn't invent this method. We simply chose to work with the people who've practiced it for decades, and to make sure the value of that work reaches them directly.

"When you serve our coffee, you're serving a cup with a place, a people, and a practice behind it — not just a bean."

The farmers. Our coffee is grown by tribal smallholder farmers across the hill villages of Koraput, many cultivating less than two acres of mixed forest garden. We buy directly from these farmers and farmer collectives — no auction floors, no anonymous aggregation — which means full traceability for you, and a meaningfully larger share of the price reaching the grower.

Shade-grown, by necessity and by design. The forest canopy slows cherry maturation, concentrating sugars and flavour in the bean — a naturally sweeter, lower-acid, more balanced cup than fast-grown, sun-exposed Arabica. It's also better for the land: shade cultivation preserves topsoil, supports pollinators, and keeps these hill farms productive season after season.

FAQ

Frequently asked — B2B orders

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ for business accounts starts at 5kg per order. Smaller trial quantities (1kg) are available for new accounts, and sample kits are available for anyone who wants to taste before committing to a bulk order.

Do you provide GST invoices?

Yes. Every order for a registered business is accompanied by a proper GST-compliant tax invoice, so your accounts team has everything it needs for compliance and input credit.

Can I request a sample before placing a bulk order?

Absolutely — we recommend it. Our sample kits let you and your team, or your baristas, taste the coffee before committing to a larger order or a custom roast profile.

What is the shelf life of your coffee?

Whole bean stays at peak flavour for 6–9 months from the roast date when stored cool and dry, and remains good to use well beyond that. Ground coffee is best used within 2–3 months of roasting. Every bag is labelled with its roast date.

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives — do you roast to order?

Yes, we roast in small batches to order rather than holding speculative stock. Your coffee typically ships within days of roasting, not weeks or months — a meaningful difference in cup quality versus pre-roasted retail stock.

Can we set up recurring or automated deliveries?

Yes. Once we understand your average consumption, we can set up a recurring delivery schedule — weekly, biweekly, or monthly — and proactively flag restocks before you run low.

Do you offer credit terms for businesses?

Qualified business accounts with a consistent order history can move from upfront payment to invoice-based credit terms. Get in touch with our team to discuss eligibility.

Do you deliver pan-India, or only in specific regions?

We currently deliver across India for bulk business orders. Delivery frequency and lead times vary by location — let us know your city in your inquiry and we'll confirm exact timelines.
